Process

From first call
to shipped system.

  1. 1

    Discover

    One to two weeks inside your business. I talk to the people doing the work, map the data, and find the use cases with the best effort-to-impact ratio.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    A working proof of concept in your hands within weeks, not quarters — tested against your real data so we know it works before we invest further.

  3. 3

    Ship

    Hardening, evals, security review, and integration into your existing stack. It goes live with monitoring — not a demo that dies in a drawer.

  4. 4

    Scale

    I hand over clean documentation, train your team, and stay available as the system grows. You own everything — code, prompts, and know-how.
